The Journey To The Crown

From registration to the crown — here's exactly how The Crown Hunt journey works.

1

Register Online

Fill the registration form on our website with your details and preferred audition city. Registration is completely free.

2

Round 1 — City-Level Audition

Visit your chosen city on the scheduled date. This round judges physical presence and confidence — dress code: Blue Denim/Shorts with black shirt/T-shirt/kurti, or a black one-piece.

3

Round 2 — Personal Interaction & Jury Round

Candidates who clear Round 1 move to Round 2, a deeper evaluation with the jury — a one-on-one interaction assessing communication skills, confidence, and personality beyond first impressions, before final shortlisting.

4

Final Shortlisting — Top 50 + 50

50 male and 50 female contestants are shortlisted from across all five zones to advance to the grooming stage.

5

Grooming Session (5–7 Days)

Shortlisted contestants go through an intensive grooming program covering confidence, presentation, and industry readiness.

6

Portfolio Shoot & Video Profile

Professional indoor and outdoor portfolio photoshoot, along with a video profile for each contestant.

7

Grand Finale

Finalists from all five zones compete on one final stage in September 2026 for the title of Mr. & Miss Haryana 2026.

8

Industry Placement

Winners receive 100% guaranteed industry placement with mandatory training provided for each project — real entry into acting, modeling, and the pageant industry, not just assistance.

Eligibility

Any candidate aged 17–35, male or female, can register.

Registration Fee

Free — no fee at any stage of registration or audition.

Dress Code (Round 1)

Blue Denim/Shorts with black shirt/T-shirt/kurti, or a black one-piece.

Judging Criteria

Physical presence, confidence, and how naturally you carry yourself — be genuine, don't overshow.
