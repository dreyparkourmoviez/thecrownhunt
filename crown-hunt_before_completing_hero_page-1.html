About Us

The Crown Hunt is Haryana's first-of-its-kind beauty and talent pageant, created to give the state's youth a genuine, credible platform to be discovered. Starting with city-level auditions across Rohtak, Hisar, Gurgaon, Chandigarh, and Noida, and culminating in a Grand Finale, we search for Haryana's next Mr. & Miss Haryana 2026 — individuals with confidence, presence, and star quality. Produced by DreyParkour Moviez, The Crown Hunt goes beyond the usual pageant format: every selected finalist gets real exposure to the modeling, acting, and pageant industry, backed by our 100% guaranteed industry placement promise.

Vision

Our vision is to build a fair, transparent, and accessible stage where talent — not background or connections — decides who rises. We want to shine a light on hidden talent from every corner of Haryana, from big cities to small towns, and turn that talent into real opportunity. By combining local audition access with national-level exposure, media coverage, and industry placement, The Crown Hunt aims to become Haryana's most trusted name in discovering and launching the state's next generation of models, actors, and pageant icons.

OUR MISSION

The Crown Hunt's mission is to discover and uplift talent from every corner of Haryana — from big cities to small towns — who don't usually get a fair medium to showcase themselves. We give every participant a genuine, transparent platform to compete, grow, and be seen.

Unlike typical pageants that stop at a crown and a certificate, The Crown Hunt is built on 100% guaranteed industry placement — real entry into acting, modeling, and pageant-industry opportunities, not just assistance. We believe talent should be judged on confidence, authenticity, and presence — not background or connections.

Through city-level auditions, media exposure, and a Grand Finale on a national stage, we aim to build not just winners, but confident individuals ready for real careers in the entertainment and pageant industry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can register for The Crown Hunt?

Any candidate aged 17–35, male or female, can register for Mr. & Miss Haryana 2026.

Where are the auditions held?

Five zone auditions: Rohtak, Hisar, Gurgaon, Chandigarh, Noida. Rohtak and Hisar completed; rest upcoming.

When and where is the Grand Finale?

August 2026, Chandigarh/Gurgaon (exact date and venue to be finalized).

What do winners get?

100% guaranteed industry placement, not just assistance — real entry into acting, modeling, and pageant-industry opportunities.

Produced by DreyParkour MOVIEZ.

Is there a registration fee?

No, registrations are free.
